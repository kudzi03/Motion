# VelaBuilt — brag plan

Input: https://velabuilt.com (live site). Format: **vertical 1080×1920, 30fps** (the brief came from an Instagram Reel, and VelaBuilt's own channel is Instagram). Tone: **polished**: serious, restrained, premium, paced by the site's own "cinematic" ease `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`.

## Rubric

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | VelaBuilt, a creative technology studio that designs and builds premium websites, AI agents, business automation and operational systems (CRMs, dashboards). |
| Who is it for, what does it do for them? | Businesses that have outgrown their site and lose enquiries between tools. The website brings the enquiry in, and the systems behind it make sure it is answered, followed up and recorded. |
| What sets it apart? | The four layers are built as one connected system. It is also unusually honest: its agent "says so when it does not know", a person reads every enquiry, and the site has a "What this site does not claim" section. |
| Most impressive claim | "Vela, the voice on this site, is one of them." Their own AI guide is the proof. |
| Visual hook | The wireframe sphere with the amber core, plus the huge expanded-width Archivo headline. |
| Real UI / flow to show | The hero; Cardio Life (real delivered work); the Talk to Vela panel; the day one / day three / day ten follow-up; the CRM stages. |
| Tone | Polished. |
| Share caption | "We build the systems a business runs on." One enquiry, from the website to the CRM. |

## Angle

**One enquiry, followed through the whole system.** The site's own FAQ says it: *"the website brings the enquiry in, and the systems behind it make sure it is answered, followed up and recorded."* Each scene is one of VelaBuilt's four pillars doing its job, using their section numbers, headlines and UI.

Guardrails: the site states that it publishes no client results it cannot evidence and makes no performance, revenue or ranking guarantees. So the video contains **no invented metrics, testimonials or client outcomes**. Demo screens follow the site's own convention and carry a small `SYSTEM DEMO` tag.

Reading budget: every big headline is 1–3 words and lands first. The sentence under it is 5–7 words and holds 2s or more. In-UI key lines (the Vela answer, "stopped the moment someone replies") hold 1.5s or more once complete.

## Music cue guidance

Original score composed in code for this video: no third-party music, so no licence risk for VelaBuilt's own channels. D major, 120 BPM, warm and restrained: pad and sub swell in the intro; the groove (soft four-on-floor, offbeat hats, sidechained bass, maj9 chord stabs, pluck arp) enters on the 3.0s downbeat; a lift at 11.5s; an impact on the 19.0s downbeat, then drums drop out and the final Dmaj9 rings to the end. SFX sit under the music, tuned to the key.
