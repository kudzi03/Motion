# VelaBuilt showreel v2: plan

**Principle:** proof → capability → business outcome → VelaBuilt. Show the work; say almost nothing.
**Format:** 1080×1920, 30fps, 25.0s. Master rendered supersampled (composition at 2× device pixels, downscaled with Lanczos) so thin lines and small UI text survive platform compression.
**Identity kept:** cream `#f4f1ec` and ink `#151412`, champagne and amber accents, Archivo expanded display, Geist Mono labels, the VelaBuilt mark, and the wireframe sphere. Real work sits inside rounded "windows" framed by cream, so the brand holds while the work is shown large.

## Cut list (things removed from v1)

- The single-question Vela panel. Replaced by an agent that visibly qualifies and books.
- The four-card CRM recap. Replaced by the real DreamBuilder CRM, follow-up and booking screens.
- The page-counter chrome during the proof section. It distracts from the work.
